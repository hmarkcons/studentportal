1. Position

The Company employs the Employee as {{designation}}. The Employee will carry out the duties of that position, and any reasonable related duties Management assigns, honestly and to the best of their ability.

2. Probation

4. Salary

The Company will pay the Employee a monthly salary of {{monthly_salary}}, subject to the deductions in clause 5 and to any tax the law requires the Company to withhold.

5. Deductions for absence and late arrival

Deductions are calculated from the attendance system's records and shown on the Employee's monthly payroll:

  1. Absence. A working day on which the Employee has no attendance record is an absence, and is deducted at one day's salary — the monthly salary divided by the number of scheduled working days in that month.
  2. Late arrival. Arriving after {{entry_time}} plus the {{grace_minutes}}-minute grace period is late. Late time is deducted by the minute, at the Employee's hourly rate — one day's salary divided by the number of hours in their working day. Arriving within the grace period is not late.
  3. Deductions are shown on each month's payroll, and the Employee may raise any disagreement with Management within seven (7) days of receiving it.

6. Confidentiality and data protection

In the course of their work the Employee will handle students' personal information and the Company's business information. The Employee agrees that:

  • students' personal details, documents, passports, academic records, visa matters and fees are confidential, and will be used only to do the Employee's work;
  • they will not copy, share, forward or disclose any such information outside the Company, or to anyone inside it who does not need it, without the Company's written permission;
  • their login details to the Company's portal and systems are personal to them and will not be shared with anyone;
  • on leaving, they will return all Company property, documents and devices, and keep no copies; and
  • these duties continue after this Agreement ends, however it ends.

7. Notice period

After confirmation, either party may end this Agreement by giving the other one (1) month's written notice. The Company may instead pay one month's salary in place of notice. Nothing in this clause limits the Company's right to end the Agreement without notice for gross misconduct.

8. General

This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties about the Employee's employment, and replaces any earlier arrangement. Any change to it must be in writing and signed by both parties. It is governed by the laws of Pakistan.
