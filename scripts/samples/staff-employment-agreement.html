4. Salary

The Company will pay the Employee a monthly salary of {{monthly_salary}}, subject to the deductions in clause 5 and to any tax the law requires the Company to withhold.

5. Deductions for absence and late arrival

Deductions are calculated from the attendance system's records and shown on the Employee's monthly payroll:

  1. Absence. A working day on which the Employee has no attendance record, and which is not approved leave under clause 6, is an absence, and is deducted at one day's salary — the monthly salary divided by the number of scheduled working days in that month.
  2. Late arrival. Arriving after {{entry_time}} plus the {{grace_minutes}}-minute grace period is late. Late time is deducted by the minute, at the Employee's hourly rate — one day's salary divided by the number of hours in their working day. Arriving within the grace period is not late.
  3. Deductions are shown on each month's payroll, and the Employee may raise any disagreement with Management within seven (7) days of receiving it.

6. Leave

  1. The Employee is entitled to fourteen (14) days of paid leave in each year of employment, counted from the date of this Agreement. This one allowance covers planned, sick and emergency leave.
  2. Planned leave must be requested in writing at least one (1) month in advance, and may be taken only once Management has approved it in writing.
  3. Sick or emergency leave must be reported to Management on the day, before the entry time where possible, and supported by a medical certificate. It is taken from the fourteen (14) days.
  4. Leave not taken by the end of a year lapses. It does not carry over into the next year and is not paid out.
  5. Approved leave is paid, and is not deducted under clause 5. Any other day away from work — leave taken without approval, sick or emergency leave without a medical certificate, or leave taken after the fourteen (14) days are used — is an unpaid absence under clause 5.

7. Code of conduct

The Employee agrees to the following standards throughout their employment.

Professional behaviour

  • Act honestly and with integrity in all work for the Company.
  • Treat students, their families, colleagues and partner institutions with courtesy and respect.
  • Not harass, bully or discriminate against anyone on any ground.

Dealing with students

  • Give students accurate and complete information about universities, programmes, fees, scholarships and visa requirements.
  • Never guarantee a student an admission, a scholarship or a visa.
  • Never ask for or accept money, gifts or favours from a student or their family, other than the Company's official fees paid to the Company.

Conflict of interest

  • Not work for, advise or receive payment from another consultancy, agent or institution while employed by the Company, without its written permission.
  • Not advise or process any student privately, or refer the Company's students elsewhere.
  • Tell Management at once of any personal interest that could conflict with their duties.

Dress, punctuality and devices

  • Dress professionally at work and at any event where they represent the Company.
  • Arrive on time and keep to the working hours in clause 3.
  • Keep personal phone and social media use during working hours to a minimum, and never post students' information or the Company's confidential information online.

A breach of this code may lead to a written warning or, if serious, to the end of this Agreement under clause 9.

8. Confidentiality and data protection

In the course of their work the Employee will handle students' personal information and the Company's business information. The Employee agrees that:

  • students' personal details, documents, passports, academic records, visa matters and fees are confidential, and will be used only to do the Employee's work;
  • they will not copy, share, forward or disclose any such information outside the Company, or to anyone inside it who does not need it, without the Company's written permission;
  • their login details to the Company's portal and systems are personal to them and will not be shared with anyone;
  • on leaving, they will return all Company property, documents and devices, and keep no copies; and
  • these duties continue after this Agreement ends, however it ends.

9. Notice period

After confirmation, either party may end this Agreement by giving the other one (1) month's written notice. The Company may instead pay one month's salary in place of notice. Nothing in this clause limits the Company's right to end the Agreement without notice for gross misconduct.

10. General

This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties about the Employee's employment, and replaces any earlier arrangement. Any change to it must be in writing and signed by both parties. It is governed by the laws of Pakistan.
